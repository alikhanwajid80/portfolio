About

Idea to delivery, one person handling the whole reel.

Wajid Ali Khan is a Doha-based videographer, photographer, and editor with seven years turning briefs into footage people actually watch. The work runs the full length of a production — concept and research, shooting on his own camera, gimbal, and drone, then cutting, grading, and delivering the final file.

Clients range from cafés and restaurants to real estate developers, cleaning companies, gyms, and gaming arenas across Qatar — each project shaped by the industry it's made for, not a single house style.
